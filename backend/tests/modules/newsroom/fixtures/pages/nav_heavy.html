PMRDA issues notices for ring road land

The Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority has issued notices to landowners in 12 villages for the acquisition of land needed for the Pune ring road, an official said on Monday.

The notices cover about 420 hectares along the proposed 128 km alignment, and landowners have been given 30 days to file objections with the district collector office.

The authority has said compensation will be paid at rates fixed under the land acquisition law, and that an independent valuer will verify the market value before awards are declared.

Officials added that joint measurement of the land will begin next week in the villages nearest to Wagholi and that farmers will be informed by a written intimation.
