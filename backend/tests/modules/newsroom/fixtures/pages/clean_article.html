Kharadi bypass diversion from October 1

By Staff Reporter

Pune traffic police have diverted vehicles on the Kharadi bypass from October 1 to October 20 to allow girder launching work on the new flyover.

Heavy vehicles coming from Solapur Road will be turned towards the Hadapsar-Kharadi link, while light vehicles can use the service road on both sides, officials said on Tuesday.

The flyover is being built by the Pune Municipal Corporation at a cost of Rs 160 crore. A senior police officer said the diversion would be reviewed every five days depending on traffic volumes at the EON IT Park junction.

Residents of Kharadi and Wagholi have been advised to leave early on weekday mornings. Traffic wardens will be posted at the Kharadi bypass chowk between 8 am and 11 am.
