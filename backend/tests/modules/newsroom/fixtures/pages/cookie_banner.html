A new school campus will open in Wagholi from June with classes from nursery to grade 8, the trust running the school said on Friday at an event attended by local residents.

The campus is spread over 3 acres near the Wagholi-Kesnand road and has been designed for 1,200 students, with a playground, a library and a science laboratory, the trust said.

Admissions for the first batch open on January 15, and the school has announced that 25 per cent of seats will be reserved under the Right to Education Act as required by the state.

The trust also said a bus service will connect the campus with Kharadi and Viman Nagar, with fees for transport to be announced before the admissions begin next year.
