## Post

Most property portals sell the top spot. We're building the opposite: a network of Pune agents buyers can check.

At Avasetu we read every MahaRERA registration in Pune again and again: completion dates, how many homes are booked,
which dates have moved. Buyers use our project pages to check a project before they visit.

What an agent gets today:

✅ Your own page with your name, photo and MahaRERA agent number, and we check that number against the register
✅ Type a project's MahaRERA number and your listing fills itself: sizes, possession date, location
✅ Posts and a short video for each listing, made for you. Nothing goes out until you approve it
✅ Our research on the projects you sell, to send to your buyers

What we're building with the first agents:

✅ A lead board of Pune buyers: what they want, their budget band, the pages they read. No names or numbers until you're picked
✅ Ask for the buyers that fit you. The agent who knows the project and calls fastest gets the buyer

Three rules we won't break:
1. Nobody can pay for a higher place.
2. No made-up reviews or deals.
3. A buyer's number is shared only with their consent.

Joining is open and free. If you sell homes anywhere in Pune, comment "Pune" or message me.
