# Avasetu agent guide

**Show Avasetu to a Pune agent, sign him up, get his first listing and first lead, and check in after a week.**

Written for you, the owner, to follow on your phone. Every step marked ✅ was run on the live site
(https://34-180-39-243.sslip.io) on 2 October 2026 with test numbers. The screenshots come from that run, and all the test data was
deleted afterwards. Steps marked ⚠️ were **not** run. Read them as instructions only, not as tested.

Our site address is `https://34-180-39-243.sslip.io`. Below it is shortened to **the site** (for example, "the site/for-agents").

---

## One-page checklist

**Before the meeting**
- [ ] Phone charged and on mobile data. Open the site/for-agents once so it loads fast later.
- [ ] Know his WhatsApp number and his main areas.

**Showcase (5 minutes, Part A)**
- [ ] the site/for-agents → the site/agent/demo → a listing on it and its chat → the site/agent/avasetu → the site/go → the site/news
- [ ] Ask: "Shall I make your page now? It takes 10 minutes."

**Onboard (Part B). Pick one way**
- [ ] **You do it for him:** Studio → Agents → + Add agent → send him the WhatsApp message → Brand → Add listing for him → he agrees on WhatsApp → switch Consent on → Preview his page → Post to Avasetu
- [ ] **He does it himself:** run `invite.ps1` for his number → send him the code → he opens the site/join → creates his site → My brand → + Add listing
- [ ] Collect: logo, banner, his photo, colour, business name, MahaRERA agent number, areas, languages, 3 to 6 photos per home, home details, about-the-project facts, consent on WhatsApp

**First listing and first lead (Part C)**
- [ ] Listing published, marketing pack opened, page shared on WhatsApp
- [ ] He knows where leads appear: Home badge, **Leads** tab, **Interest** tab

**Day 7 check-in (Part D)**
- [ ] 10-minute call and the tracking-sheet row filled in
- [ ] **Success = he adds a second listing within 7 days, without being asked.**

## Part B. Onboard an agent

### What to collect from him (send this as one WhatsApp message)

> Namaste {name} ji, to set up your Avasetu page please send:
> 1. Your **logo** (square) and a **banner** photo (wide: your office or a skyline is fine)
> 2. A clear **photo of yourself**
> 3. Your **business name** and a **colour** you like (or your logo's colour)
> 4. One line about you (**tagline**) and 2 to 3 lines **about you** (years, areas, what you are known for). No phone numbers or links in these.
> 5. Your **MahaRERA agent registration number** (it starts with A)
> 6. The **areas** you work in (up to 6 in Pune) and the **languages** you speak
> 7. For each home: locality and project name, BHK, carpet area, price, possession, floor, furnishing, amenities, **3 to 6 real photos**,
>    and what buyers always ask (parking, maintenance, water, power backup, nearby schools and offices)
> 8. Please reply **"I agree"** to this: *I agree that Avasetu may feature me and my listings on the Avasetu Facebook Page, Instagram and website, with my name and RERA number, and that buyers' interest comes to my inbox.*

Rules the app enforces (you will see an error if you break them): text fields reject phone numbers, links and e-mail addresses. The
business name can be up to 60 characters and the tagline up to 90. Areas must be in Pune. The RERA number is "A" followed by digits.
We show it as "stated by the agent" and never call him "verified".

## Part C. His first listing and first leads ✅

### Add the listing (about 1 minute)
1. Studio → **+ Add listing**. He types it the way he would say it, for example:
   *"2 BHK Kharadi 85 lakh ready to move, 950 sq ft carpet, 7th floor, parking, gym"*
   Then he taps **Add photos** and picks 2 to 6 photos, and taps **Next**. The AI read this in about 8 seconds.
   <br><img src="brand/avasetu/guide/a05-listing-typed.jpg" width="200" alt="Type it">
2. **About the project and area** (optional, but it helps the chat answer buyers): tap **Suggest from my description and the area guide**,
   check the suggestions (green "Area guide" means the line came from our guide, blue "You said" means it came from his words),
   tap **Keep all** or **Remove** on any line that is not true, then tap **Continue**. In the test it kept 11 lines (WTC Pune, EON, metro
   "approved, not running yet", parking and so on).
   <br><img src="brand/avasetu/guide/a05-about-step.jpg" width="200" alt="About step"> <img src="brand/avasetu/guide/a06-about-suggested.jpg" width="200" alt="Suggestions">
3. **Check and confirm:** the draft had Kharadi, 2 BHK, 950 sq ft, floor 7, ₹85 L and Ready. Fix anything that is wrong. If the price looks
   unusual, tick "Yes, this price is correct". Then tap **Confirm & post**. You see "Your property is ready". *(4 s)*
   <br><img src="brand/avasetu/guide/a07-review-top.jpg" width="200" alt="Review"> <img src="brand/avasetu/guide/a07-published.jpg" width="200" alt="Published">

## Part D. First-week check-in (day 7, 10 minutes)

Ask these questions and write down the answers:
1. Did you post a listing? How long did it take? What confused you?
2. Did you share the cards? Where (WhatsApp Status, groups, Instagram, Facebook)?
3. Did anyone enquire? How (chat, form, comment, WhatsApp)? Was the summary useful?
4. What would make you post your **next** home here, and not only on WhatsApp?
5. If this saved you an hour a day, what would you pay per month?

**Success measure:** he adds a **second listing within 7 days** of the first, **without being asked**.
Check it in Studio → Agents (the ring and "3 listings" item) or on his page.

**Tracking sheet (one row per agent):** Agent | Area | Invited on | First listing on | Listings after 14 days | Photos on listings |
Enquiries | Site visits | Second listing within 7 days (Y/N) | Main complaint | Would pay (₹/month)

## Part E. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| **"Incorrect code"** ✅ | He typed the code wrong. He should clear the box and type the 6 digits again. (Before 2 October the app showed "Request failed (400)" here instead. That is fixed.) <br><img src="brand/avasetu/guide/a01-join-wrong-code.jpg" width="180" alt="Incorrect code"> |
| **"Too many wrong attempts"** or **"This invite is locked"** ⚠️ | After 5 wrong codes the number is locked for 30 minutes. After 15 it is locked until you send a new code. Give him a new code with `.\deploy\gcp\invite.ps1 -Phone 98XXXXXXXX`, or use **Make a new code** in Studio → Agents. The old code stops working. (Not tested: only one wrong code was tried.) |
| **"Signup is by invitation"** ✅ | That number has no invite. Check that you typed his number correctly in the invite, and issue one. |
| **The page jumps back after "Send OTP"** ✅ | The page had not finished loading. Wait a second and tap again. |
| **His page does not show a change** ✅ | Pages refresh about 30 seconds after a change, and the **first** visit after a change can still show the old page. Wait a minute, then reload. In the test, a new logo and listing appeared on a later visit. The listing page and Studio update at once. |
| **Photo upload fails** ⚠️ | Use JPG or PNG photos taken with the phone camera, and add a few at a time. The app makes them smaller before upload. On weak mobile data, try again on Wi-Fi. If one keeps failing, take a screenshot of the error and send it to the developer. (Uploads worked in the test: a logo and 2 photos for each listing.) |
| **"Request failed (409)" on Post to Avasetu** ✅ | Fixed on 2 October: the app now makes the missing marketing pack itself. If you see "Record the agent's consent", switch Consent on first. |
| **No leads** | Check that the listing is **live** (Listings tab). Share the **listing page** link, not only the photos. Open the chat on his listing yourself to check that it answers. Leads only come when the buyer gives his number. Questions without a number appear under **Interest → Website chats**. |
| **His post is not on Facebook or Instagram** ⚠️ | Posting goes only to the **Avasetu** Page and Instagram, never his own accounts. It needs his consent switched on and your **Approve and post**. While real posting is switched off on the server (`SOCIAL_DRY_RUN=true`), nothing actually appears and the result says "dry run". Instagram posts show "link in our bio": check the site/go. If Facebook shows a red banner in Interest, the Page token has expired: see docs/META_SETUP.md. (Not tested: nothing was posted in this run.) |
| **Site down** ✅ | the site/api/v1/health should say "healthy". If not, see docs/PILOT_RUNBOOK.md. |

### For the developer: how this guide was tested
- Script: `frontend/e2e/guide-run.js` (system Chrome, 390x844). Commands: `PHASE=self|owner|show CODE=<test code> node e2e/guide-run.js`.
- Test data helper: `backend/scripts/g1_testdata.py scan | promote <test no> | delete --confirm`. It only touches 9000000011 to 9000000014 and the G1 test names.
- Bugs found and fixed during the run: app error messages showed "Request failed (NNN)" (server error envelope not unwrapped), which
  broke Post to Avasetu on first use; the chat on a listing page said "could not find a listed home that matches" when the page's own home
  matched; the Studio badge read "New website chat lead or chat"; the server invite script printed "<your app link>" instead of the join link.
