# Avasetu copy pack (2026-10-09)

The brand idea in one line: **आवास (home) + सेतु (bridge) = the bridge between a family and the right home, and between an agent and the right buyer.**
Everything below says that one thing in different lengths. Paste-ready; character counts are within each platform's limit.
Rules kept: no "WhatsApp us" until the number is live, no fake numbers, no "only/just/केवल", sample homes are never "for sale".

## 1. The story (About page, FB "Our story", pinned post)

> **Avasetu = Avas + Setu.**
> *Avas* (आवास) is home. *Setu* (सेतु) is a bridge.
> In Pune, finding a home means crossing a river of brochures, broker calls and half-true prices.
> We build the bridge: clear price, carpet area, possession date and MahaRERA number, in plain words,
> and a trusted local agent on the other side.
> For agents, we are the bridge to serious buyers: your own website, posts and reels made for you,
> and every enquiry qualified before it reaches your phone.
> **Avasetu. Your bridge to the right home.**

Hindi:

> **आवास + सेतु = आवासेतु।**
> आवास यानी घर, सेतु यानी पुल।
> पुणे में घर ढूंढना मतलब ब्रोशर, कॉल और अधूरी कीमतों की नदी पार करना।
> हम पुल बनाते हैं: साफ़ कीमत, कारपेट एरिया, पज़ेशन की तारीख और MahaRERA नंबर, आसान शब्दों में,
> और दूसरी तरफ़ एक भरोसेमंद लोकल एजेंट।
> **आवासेतु। सही घर तक आपका सेतु।**
